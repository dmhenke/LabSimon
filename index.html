Research

THINC is a BCM center of excellence whose mission is to bridge cutting-edge RNA basic science with early-stage drug discovery. Our group supplies the computational half of that bridge: methods for complex molecular data, built to survive contact with a real therapeutic program.

RNA splicing as a therapeutic vulnerability

MYC-hyperactivated tumours place a collateral burden on the spliceosome, making core splicing factors a synthetic-lethal entry point. This line of work traced that vulnerability from a genome-wide screen to a clinical-stage drug program, and showed that spliceosome-targeted therapy also triggers an antiviral immune response in triple-negative breast cancer.

Nature 2015 · Cell 2021

Deep learning for single-cell genomics

Single-cell measurements are sparse, noisy and batch-confounded. We design neural architectures that model those artefacts explicitly — count-based denoising autoencoders, triplet networks for dataset integration, positive-unlabelled learning for empty droplets — rather than smoothing them away and hoping.

Nat Commun 2019 · Patterns 2021

Biomarker and target discovery

Functional genomic screens and clinical omics answer different questions and disagree often. We integrate them — bio-primed machine learning, cell-type deconvolution, mitochondrial variant tracing — to nominate targets and response biomarkers that hold up outside the discovery cohort.

npj Precis Oncol 2025 · Genes 2023

Multi-omic atlases of tissue and disease

Long-running collaborations map cell states in lung ageing and fibrosis, craniofacial development, Alzheimer's disease and drug-resistant lymphoma — single-cell and single-nucleus multiome data read for the regulatory programs that separate a resilient cell from a persister.

Nat Commun 2019 · Sci Adv 2025

Software

Methods are released as open source at github.com/lkmklsmn. Each tool below has an accompanying peer-reviewed paper.

INSCT

scRNA-seq integration with triplet neural networks — batch correction that scales to millions of cells.

Python

EmptyNN

Positive-unlabelled neural network that removes empty and damaged droplets from droplet-based scRNA-seq.

R · Patterns 2021

MitoTrace

Mitochondrial variant calling from scRNA-seq reads for lineage tracing and sample demultiplexing.

R · Genes 2023

DrivAER

Autoencoder-based relevance scores that rank the transcriptional programs driving a cellular trajectory.

Python · GigaScience 2020

DCA

Deep count autoencoder for scRNA-seq denoising (co-developed at Helmholtz Munich).

Python · Nat Commun 2019

SplicER

Splicing-efficiency quantification from RNA-seq, the analytic backbone of our splicing work.
